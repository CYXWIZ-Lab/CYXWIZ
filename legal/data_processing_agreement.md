## 3. Processor obligations

Processor will keep Personal Data confidential; ensure authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality; apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures; assist Controller with rights requests, security incidents, impact assessments, and regulator enquiries to the extent required and reasonable; maintain processing records where required; and notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed Personal Data Breach.

## 4. Subprocessors and transfers

Controller authorises the subprocessors listed in `[SUBPROCESSOR REGISTER OR SOW]`. Processor will give `[30]` days' prior notice of a material new or replacement subprocessor, allowing Controller to object on reasonable data-protection grounds. Processor remains responsible for its subprocessors' obligations. Cross-border transfers will use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required.

## 5. Security, audit, and deletion

Security measures are set out in `[SECURITY SCHEDULE]` and must reflect actual controls, not aspirational claims. Once Services end, Processor will return or delete Personal Data at Controller's choice within `[30/60]` days, unless law or backups require retention. Controller may audit compliance no more than once a year on reasonable notice, without disrupting operations, or rely on reasonable third-party assurance evidence where available.

## 6. Priority and survival

This DPA survives while Processor retains Personal Data. The parties will amend it where needed to meet applicable data protection law, including UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 and any law that validly applies to the processing.
