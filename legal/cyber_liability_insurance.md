# Cyber Liability Insurance Standard

Status: internal procurement standard — this is not evidence that CYXWIZ holds any insurance.

## Objective

Before accepting material client data, production access, or contractual cyber-liability obligations, CYXWIZ should obtain broker advice and insurance aligned with actual services, revenue, geographies, data categories, and contracts. Coverage must never be represented as active until a certificate and policy are on file.

## Required coverage review

Ask a UAE-authorised insurance broker to assess at least:

- cyber liability, including privacy liability, network-security liability, incident response, forensic investigation, notification, legal support, extortion, business interruption, and media liability where relevant;
- technology errors and omissions / professional indemnity for software and implementation services;
- crime/social-engineering coverage where CYXWIZ handles payments or funds; and
- general liability and employer-related covers required by law or contract.

## Underwriting disclosure

Provide accurate information: legal entity and licence, revenue, services, security controls, hosting and subprocessors, data volumes and types, countries, prior incidents/claims, contracts, and requested limits. Do not misstate capabilities, certifications, data residency, or loss history.

## Minimum policy review points

- Insured entity, subsidiaries, employees, contractors, and retroactive date
- Limits, deductibles, aggregate limits, defence costs, and sublimits
- Territorial scope and claims-made reporting requirements
- Exclusions for unencrypted devices, contractual liability, sanctions, professional services, AI, virtual assets, and regulated activity
- Approved incident-response panel, breach coach, notification, and consent requirements before spending or admitting liability
- Client contract requirements: limits, additional insured, waiver, and certificate wording

## Evidence and operational actions

Maintain a restricted insurance register with broker, insurer, policy number, term, limits, deductible, renewal date, endorsements, and certificate. Assign an owner to renew at least 60 days before expiry. Keep an incident playbook with insurer/broker reporting channels and ensure the MSA does not promise coverage or limits beyond the active policy.
