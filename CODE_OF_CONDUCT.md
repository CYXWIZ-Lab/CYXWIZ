# Code of Conduct

CyxWiz adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Its full text is the code of conduct of this project; this page says how it applies here.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in CyxWiz to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Be kind, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.

## Scope

The code of conduct applies in every CyxWiz community space: the issue tracker, pull requests and reviews, discussions, chat channels and events. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example from an official account or as a speaker.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by email to **contact@cyxwiz.ai**, with a subject that starts with `Conduct:`. Reports are read by the project maintainers, handled promptly and fairly, and kept confidential. Tell us if you are not comfortable with a particular maintainer handling your report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, chosen by the impact of the behaviour. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct.
